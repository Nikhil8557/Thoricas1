The ProblemSlide 2 of 6
02 / The Solution

Simple top-down physical interception.

Offloading tracking to the ground keeps our airborne platform cheap and agile.

1

Ground Vectoring

Ground radar computes line-of-sight and sends acceleration setpoints. No heavy, expensive computing on the drone.

2

Overhead Ingress

The interceptor dives from above, utilizing the threat drone’s own rotor downwash to suck in our payload.

3

Motor Stall Pulse

A regulated pneumatic pulse ejects high-tensile polymer that wraps motor bells, forcing immediate ESC shutdown.
