02 / The Solution

Physical interdiction in four synchronized steps.

A top-down mechanical interception mechanism engineered to turn the threat's downwash suction against itself.

01

Radar Vectoring

Ground 3D sensors track the target and send course corrections to our lightweight interceptor, keeping airborne hardware cheap and expendable.

02

Top-Down Ingress

The interceptor approaches from above the target. This exploits the hostile drone's natural downward rotor suction to pull our payload into the blades.

03

Pneumatic Pulse

A cold-gas micro-impulse deploys a high-tensile polymer fan. The planar sweep resists turbulent wake and blankets all spinning motor bells simultaneously.

04

Instant Motor Lock

Fibers bind the motor hubs in milliseconds, triggering immediate electronic speed controller (ESC) overcurrent shutdown and ground recovery.
